About SmartCompress AI
Information Storage Management (ISM) Project

SmartCompress AI is an end-to-end storage optimization platform designed to solve the critical trade-off between compression ratio, processor latency, and data fidelity in enterprise storage tiers.

Shannon Entropy
Calculates byte-level entropy $H(X) = -\sum p(x)\log_2 p(x)$ to mathematically establish compressibility limits.
Random Forest ML
Trained on empirical measured benchmark data across lossless algorithms to recommend optimal strategies.
Real Engines
Actual execution using PDF-Deflate, 7Z, ZIP, GZIP, BZIP2, and LZMA with verified bit-for-bit integrity.

Compatible with standard enterprise storage management paradigms: SAN, NAS, object stores, and archival tiers.
